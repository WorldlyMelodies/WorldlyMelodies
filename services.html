Live Music for Weddings & Funerals

Worldly Melodies provides singers and a full band for your occasion — heartfelt live music, with stage speakers set up for every performance. Singers are always included in every package.

  • $1,000 — We perform at your venue (music only, no food).
  • $1,500–$2,000 — Performance with food (price depends on the menu).
  • $2,000 — Out of town (no food).
  • $2,500 — Out of town: band, food & drink included.
  • $5,000 — We provide the venue and the food (Orange County), plus the full performance.

A $500 non-refundable down payment reserves your date. Please book at least 3 months in advance.
